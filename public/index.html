        How to play 🗣️

        1. An engine speaks a word from a real-ish language or pure gibberish.
        2. Press ▶ Play the word (replay or 🐢 slower as much as you like).
        3. Spell what you heard in the answer language. Use 🔊 Hear my guess to compare — if the host enabled it, each try stacks with its waveform so you can pick the closest.
        4. When time runs out, every guess is pronounced and scored by how close it sounds to the original. Closest sound wins the round.

        Scoring is by sound, not spelling — banana and bununa score almost the same.
